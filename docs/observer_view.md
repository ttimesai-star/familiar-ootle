## Summary

Visible: that a leash exists and its rule (limit, window, per-spend cap, the allow-listed roots), every call to it, the exact amount each payment moves out (covenant claim), the root each output is locked under, the one-time keys that sign and pay fees, and the leaf used for each spend (the errand leaf on payments, the recall leaf only when the owner recalls).

Hidden: how much the budget holds and what is left of it (every UTXO amount is a commitment), who is behind a payee root (a root is a hash of the payee's own conditions), and what the second leaf says (an owner recall key) until it is used; that the tree has a second leaf is visible as one sibling hash. With single-use payee roots (leash 2), two payments to the same shop go to two unrelated roots.

Coupon mode (leash 2): no payment publishes an amount. A payment spends whole coupons through the leaf `component(<leash>)` without a covenant claim and sends all of their value to allow-listed roots; the leash counts coupons, not uT. An observer sees how many coupons each payment spent and how many were funded, so relative sizes are visible ("this payment was 3 coupons, that one 2"); the denomination, and so every absolute amount, is not.

Leaks in this demo, by design of the demo rather than of Ootle: step 1 funds the one-time agent, owner and stranger keys from one funder account in a single transaction, so an observer links those keys to that account and to each other; the owner key also appears in the leash's access rules. A real owner funds the one-time keys from separate stealth outputs.
