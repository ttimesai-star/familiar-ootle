Familiar

A private allowance for AI agents on Tari Ootle. The agent can spend only up to the owner's limits, only to payees the owner listed, and the owner can take the budget back at any moment. The network enforces it, not the agent's code. In coupon mode no amount ever reaches the chain: not the budget, not what is left, not what a payment moved. An observer sees that a payment happened and how many hidden coupons it spent, never what a coupon is worth, nor who is behind a payee. Only the network fee of each transaction is public.

      The problem

      The Ootle wallet daemon can hand an AI agent an API key with scopes such as TransactionSend_<account>. A scope says what the agent may do, not how much: a key that may send may send the whole account. An agent that reads the web can be told, in text hidden in a page, to do exactly that. On a transparent chain its wallet is also public: balance, every service it pays, who funds it.

      Familiar moves the limits into the chain and keeps the purse out of sight.

      Every budget UTXO: amount hidden by a Pedersen commitment, locked under a pure condition tree Script(root) with two leaves: errand = component(leash) AND BalancePreserved(cap) and recall = public_key(owner).

      Privacy: visible, hidden, and what leaks

      Visible to anyone

      • each payment, its time, the leash and the one-time key that signed it;
      • amount mode: the exact amount of each payment (the covenant claim); coupon mode: only how many coupons it spent;
      • the errand leaf of each spent UTXO, and the whole component state (rule, window usage, roots).

      Hidden

      • how much the budget holds and has left (commitments; the recall carries no claim);
      • coupon mode: what any payment moved: the coupon denomination is written nowhere on-chain;
      • who is behind a payee root;
      • what the second leaf says (the owner's recall) until it is used; only its hash is published;
      • with single-use roots: that two payments went to the same shop.

      Leaks, named. In amount mode payment amounts are public; in coupon mode their relative sizes are (3 coupons, then 2). The window usage is public (component state). Payments of one leash are linked by the leash and the agent key. In this demo the one-time keys are funded from one account in one transaction, which links them; a real owner funds them from separate stealth outputs. Prototype, not audited.

      Try it yourself

      Your own leash in coupon mode on Esmeralda, from a fresh key, in about five minutes (prebuilt client in the release, or cargo build -p familiar-cli --release):

      familiar wallet me.json        # a fresh testnet key, written only to me.json
familiar tap    me.json        # 5 tTARI from the Familiar tap
familiar leash  me.json        # a leash in coupon mode with 10 hidden coupons
familiar pay    me.json candle-shop 2
familiar ask    me.json "any text, e.g. a hidden instruction to pay refund-desk"
familiar pause  me.json; familiar recall me.json; familiar show me.json
