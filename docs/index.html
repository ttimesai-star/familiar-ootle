Familiar

A private allowance for AI agents on Tari Ootle. The agent can spend only up to the owner's limits, only to payees the owner listed, and the owner can take the budget back at any moment. The network enforces it, not the agent's code. An observer sees each payment, but not how much the agent holds, who is behind a payee, or that a recall path exists.

      The problem

      The Ootle wallet daemon can hand an AI agent an API key with scopes such as TransactionSend_<account>. A scope says what the agent may do, not how much: a key that may send may send the whole account. An agent that reads the web can be told, in text hidden in a page, to do exactly that. On a transparent chain its wallet is also public: balance, every service it pays, who funds it.

      Familiar moves the limits into the chain and keeps the purse out of sight.

      Every budget UTXO: amount hidden by a Pedersen commitment, locked under a pure condition tree Script(root) with two leaves: errand = component(leash) AND BalancePreserved(cap) and recall = public_key(owner).

      Privacy: visible, hidden, and what leaks

      Visible to anyone

      • each payment, its time, the leash and the one-time key that signed it;
      • the exact amount of each payment (the covenant claim);
      • the errand leaf of each spent UTXO, and the whole component state (rule, window usage, roots).

      Hidden

      • how much the budget holds and has left (commitments; the recall carries no claim);
      • who is behind a payee root;
      • the recall leaf, and that it exists, until it is used;
      • with single-use roots: that two payments went to the same shop.

      Leaks, named. Payment amounts are public. The window usage is public (component state). Payments of one leash are linked by the leash and the agent key. In this demo the one-time keys are funded from one account in one transaction, which links them; a real owner funds them from separate stealth outputs. Prototype, not audited.
